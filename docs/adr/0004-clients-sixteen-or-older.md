# Clients must be 16 or older

Kinetiq stores health data (quiz answers, logs, check-ins), which needs explicit consent under GDPR Art. 9, and in Poland a child can give consent for an online service alone only from 16 (Art. 8). Supporting younger clients would need a verified parental consent flow, so the join form requires "I am 16 or older" plus consent to processing health data, and no birth date is stored. Trainers confirm they are 18 or older because they use Kinetiq professionally. Clients under 16 with parental consent are a backlog item.
