## Phase 1: Apply or expire at the effective Monday (#144) [US1, US2]

**Goal**: once a plan change's effective Monday has begun in the client's time zone, the system applies an accepted one to the plan (keeping the old plan as a revision) and expires every other open one, exactly once, even when late or repeated.

**Independent test**: with a fixed clock, a plan change accepted by both is not in the plan the Sunday before and is in it on the Monday, with a revision of the old plan; a pending and an accepted-by-one plan change expire and leave the plan identical; a run twice or three days late gives the same result; a plan edited at the touched entry expires with `PLAN_EDITED`.
