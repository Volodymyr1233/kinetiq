## Implementation strategy

Order is fixed by the commits: spec, #48, #49. Inside #48 the domain and its tests come first, then persistence, then the web layer, with `schema.d.ts` regenerated from the running backend before the commit. The frontend starts only from the committed types. MVP is #48 + #49 together: US1 (add) is unusable without the form, and the isolation rule of US3 is part of the listing change, not an add-on. Nothing is committed until its quality gate has passed and its browser or abuse checks have been done.
